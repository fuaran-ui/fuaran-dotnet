# Migration index — 0.105.0, the announced breaking cut (Phase 2303)

`0.105.0` is the one announced breaking release of the pre-1.0 line. The surface debts that could not ride an ordinary
draft slot are released together under this one number. The record is the `## 0.105.0` section of
[`STABILITY.md`](../../STABILITY.md). It quotes every rider's stability impact line and lists the consumer-visible
symbols. This page links the per-rider migration notes, gives the order the riders land in, and lists the consumer
raises that follow the release.

The version slot was claimed for the cut on 2026-11-21. It is frozen and released after the last rider ships, and no
rider ships outside it.

## The order of riders

Each step is ordered by dependency and by which files it shares with the steps around it.

1. **2100, 2101, 2346: landed.** These are obsoletions and the C# enum. They change no wire byte and no F#
   record, so nothing after them waits on them.
2. **2107: `Choose` and `Require`.** It adds two union cases and wire-additive corpus vectors, and its change-set is
   cross-host. It goes first among the open riders because the later riders regenerate `Generated.fs` and the
   corpus, and 2107 is the one whose change is purely additive on the wire.
3. **2413: `Select.multiple` at its default.** This is an IDL default plus a fixture moved to `lenient/`. It narrows
   the canonical form, and the regenerated `SelectSpec.Multiple` moves from `bool option` to `bool`.
4. **2811: the label widening.** This is an IDL type change on two records, with every host regenerated and the
   corpus re-emitted. It shares `idl.json`, `Vocabulary.fs` and the `Select` rows with 2413, so the two land one
   after the other, never together.
5. **2835 and 2836: `AgAdapterOptions` and the removals.** 2835 drops `AgAdapterOptions`' unused type parameter;
   2836 deletes the six names that 2100 and 2101 obsoleted. Both are F# API only and landed together.
6. **2110 and 2340: not in this cut.** See the note under the table above.
7. **The freeze and the release.** The slot is frozen once every rider has shipped, then released and published,
   and the registry is checked.

## Consumer hosts outside this repository

These changes go with the cut but land in their own repositories:

- **fuaran-kt:** the public `MountDirection.InOnly` constant is removed. The decoder already refuses `"InOnly"`.
- **fuaran-swift:** the five union-case closure slots that Phase 2331 left out are added:
  `Action.ReadFileBody.onRead`, and `CellKindErased.Editable.onEdit`, `.Checkbox.onToggle`, `.Button.onClick`
  and `.Progress.labelFn`.
- **fuaran-ts:** the TypeScript half of each wire-visible rider (2107, 2413, 2811). Its `STABILITY.md` names
  the package versions for the cut.

## Consumer raises

Every downstream consumer that compiles against `Fuaran.UI.*` raises its pin to `0.105.0` after the release. Each
raise is an adoption act in the consumer's own repository: the consumer makes the edits in the notes above, rebuilds,
and records the raise there. None is performed by the release. The public playground, `fuaran-live`, is one of these
consumers. It currently pins an earlier release. Consumers that pin `0.104.0` move one minor, and those still on the
`0.99`–`0.103` lines also take the intermediate releases' notes, which `STABILITY.md` records.

## Verifying a consumer

Build the consumer against `0.105.0` with warnings as errors. The obsoletions of step 1 and the removals of step 5
both ship in `0.105.0`, so no released version carries the obsoletion warnings. A consumer finds the removed names as
FS0039 errors and takes each replacement from the 2100/2101 note. An exhaustive-match warning (FS0025) marks each
`Action` match that needs 2107's arms.
